4. COLOUR SCIENCE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Clip Scout's built-in Apple Log to Rec.709 conversion is Clip Scout's own LUT,
calculated from the Apple Log curve that Apple publishes for developers. No
Apple files are included. Camera makers' own LUTs (Sony, Canon, Blackmagic,
Panasonic, Nikon, DJI, Apple) are not included with Clip Scout: Clip Scout
uses the ones already installed with DaVinci Resolve or downloaded by you.
