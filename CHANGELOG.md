# Changelog

## 1.2.0 · 27 November 2026

**New: Colorgrade, Your PowerGrades**
- Apply to All Timeline: a clean 8-node grade on every clip (Noise Reduction, Exposure, White Balance, Primaries / Contrast, Power Window, Skintone, CST, LUT).
- Camera profiles for the CST: Sony S-Log3 / S-Log2, DJI D-Log, Canon Log 2 / 3, Apple Log, Rec.709, or Auto.
- Top 5 favourite LUTs, one click each. Apply and import PowerGrades (.drx) with previews.
- Each clip gets a new grade version, so Undo brings the old grade back.

**New: AI Smart Bins**
- People, the couple, crowds and scenes sorted into real Resolve bins. Name people from their face; names work in Search.

**New**
- Library is the first tab, with a clear "Add a project" guide.
- Timeline switcher in the Resolve status box.
- Time saved per project, for the last 30 days or all time.
- Clean up: free disk space from data Clip Scout can make again.
- How to Use: a full guide for every tool, and how to exclude clips (disabled clips, turned-off or locked tracks, "_skip" folders).
- Updates: a clear "Update available" banner, and a Restart button after installing.

## 1.1.0 · 26 November 2026

**New: Smart Cut**
- Remove pauses and filler words (umm, uh) from your timeline in one click, into a tightened copy.
- Sync camera clips to your audio recorder (Zoom, Tascam, DJI Mic, lavalier) by their sound.
- Retake finder: groups takes of the same shot and suggests the best one.

**Improved**
- Live indexing progress: percent, which clip, and a clear message if something goes wrong.
- Faster first start after installing; no console windows flashing on Windows.
- Pick your own LUTs with your computer's own file browser.
- Show all clips now loads every clip as you scroll.
- The Editor gnome joins the Time saved family.
- Update notices inside the app.

**Fixed**
- Indexing could stop right after pressing Start.

## 1.0.0 · 26 November 2026

First release: search by picture and speech, shaky footage finder, Gem Reel, Log to Rec.709, titles, library and tags, time saved.
